Xucko — Sports Intelligence That Connects the Dots

Overview

XUCKO is an AI-assisted sports intelligence platform designed to connect player and team data, uncover potentially useful patterns, estimate outcomes, and explain the evidence behind predictions.

Problem

Sports research is fragmented across player statistics, role changes, injuries, matchups, weather, and other contextual factors. Users need a consistent way to evaluate evidence and uncertainty.
